Get To Know Me

About
Me

Based in Ilorin, Nigeria · Available to Travel

I am a Nigerian beauty and commercial model, with passion for beauty, fashion, culture, and visual storytelling. I specialize in beauty, fashion, bridal, lifestyle, and commercial modeling, bringing elegance, confidence, and versatility to every project I take on.

As a model and beauty content creator, I enjoy bringing creative concepts to life while creating visuals that connect brands with their audience. My work spans skincare, hair, lashes, nails, fashion, bridal, and lifestyle, allowing me to work across different aesthetics and creative directions.

I am passionate about building meaningful collaborations with brands, photographers, creatives, and businesses that value strong visual identity and authentic storytelling. I approach every project with professionalism, attention to detail, and a commitment to delivering imagery that represents both the creative vision and the brand.

Available for modeling, commercial campaigns, brand shoots, UGC, and creative collaborations across Nigeria.
